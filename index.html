Our Story

The garland at the threshold

In Indian homes, celebrations begin with a Toran — a simple garland at the doorway, welcoming joy, blessings, and new beginnings. Torana Studio was born from that very feeling of home.

Two lifelong friends, one in India and the other in the United States, brought together by a shared love for Indian heritage, traditional artistry, and meaningful celebrations. We wanted to bring a little piece of our roots into homes everywhere.

At Torana Studio, we thoughtfully design and curate bespoke decor inspired by India's rich cultural traditions. Our lightweight, eco-conscious, easy-to-assemble creations blend timeless artistry with modern design, allowing families anywhere in the world to bring their celebrations to life themselves and make every milestone special.

Just like the Toran that welcomes joy at the doorway, we hope every Torana creation marks the beginning of something beautiful.

Questions

Before you ask

When exactly do you launch?

We are finishing the founding collection now and will open the store in phases. The founding list gets access first — often a week or two before anyone else.

Where do you ship?

Across the United States and India at launch, with Canada and the UK close behind. Kits are flat-packed specifically to keep shipping light and the carbon footprint low.

What is actually inside a kit?

The printed cloth backdrop, a tool-free hanging system, the styling props for that theme, and a printed step-by-step guide. Nothing you need to buy separately, nothing you need to drill.

Is it really tool-free?

Yes. The hanging system works on standard walls and stands without nails, ladders or professional help. If you can hang a curtain, you can host a mandap-worthy backdrop.

How does renting work?

Statement backdrops and reusable props arrive a few days before your event and go back afterwards with a prepaid label. Everything is professionally cleaned between homes.

Can I reuse a kit for another occasion?

That is the whole point. The cloth is washable and the prop systems are modular, so one kit can carry a baby shower in spring and Diwali in autumn.
