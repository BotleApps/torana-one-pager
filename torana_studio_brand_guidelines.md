# Torana Studio — Official Brand Guidelines & Voice Framework

---

## 1. Brand Soul & Essence

### 1.1 Brand Story & Name Etymology
In Indian heritage, the *Toran* (or *Torana*) is the mango-leaf garland tied at the entrance of a threshold—the very first decorative element that signals the arrival of auspiciousness, joy, and celebration. It marks the precise boundary where an ordinary space becomes a sanctuary of festivities. 

**Torana Studio** was born from this exact emotion. Founded by two lifelong friends—one based in India and the other in the United States—Torana Studio bridges geographical boundaries through a single shared thread: a deep passion for traditional Indian decor art. We craft and curate lightweight, eco-conscious, DIY traditional backdrops and decor properties that families across the globe can easily assemble to celebrate life’s cherished milestones—from weddings and Mehendi ceremonies to baby showers, birthdays, and grand festivals.

> *"Where every celebration begins."*

---

### 1.2 Mission & Vision
* **Mission:** To empower global Indian families to celebrate their traditions with authenticity, grace, and ease through beautiful, sustainable, DIY backdrop and decor kits shipped directly to their doors.
* **Vision:** To become the premier global home-celebration brand that keeps Indian cultural heritage alive, accessible, and eco-consciously celebrated across generations and borders.

---

### 1.3 Core Brand Values
1. **Authentic Heritage (*Parampara*):** Respecting time-honored Indian motifs, art forms (such as Pichwai, Kalamkari, Muggu/Kolam, and Aipan), and traditional ceremonial symbolism.
2. **Warmth & Hospitality (*Atithi Devo Bhava*):** Treating every customer as a welcome guest entering our home, ensuring every interaction feels personal, respectful, and joyful.
3. **Effortless Empowerment (*Svayam*):** Designing decor kits that allow families to experience the pride and happiness of creating their own festive backdrops together without professional setup hassle.
4. **Mindful Eco-Consciousness (*Prakriti*):** Choosing high-quality cloth textiles, reusable properties, and sustainable materials over single-use plastics and disposable decor.

## 4. Typography & Font Hierarchy

The typography pairs classic editorial luxury with clean modern legibility, echoing traditional Indian calligraphy and architectural proportions.

### 4.1 Typography Hierarchy

* **Primary Serif (Headings, Taglines, Section Titles):**
  * **Font Family:** `Cinzel` / `Cormorant Garamond` / `Playfair Display`
  * **Character:** Elegant, editorial, regal, timeless.
  * **Usage:** Logo sub-text, hero banners, section headers.

* **Secondary Sans-Serif (Body Text, Navigation, UI Elements):**
  * **Font Family:** `Montserrat` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter`
  * **Character:** Clean, highly legible, modern, friendly.
  * **Usage:** Body paragraphs, product descriptions, website UI, instructional guides.

* **Accent Script / Traditional Serif (Cultural Quotes & Callouts):**
  * **Font Family:** `Alex Brush` / `Italianno` or classical serif italics (`Cormorant Garamond Italic`)
  * **Character:** Flowing, personal, artistic.
  * **Usage:** Highlighted quotes, decorative intro phrases, thank-you cards.

## 5. Imagery & Photography Guidelines

### 5.1 Mood & Atmosphere
* **Lighting:** Warm, soft, natural sunlight streaming through courtyards or windows. Avoid harsh flash or artificially blue indoor lighting.
* **Styling:** Layered, textured, and rich. Combine cloth printed backdrops with brass *urli* bowls, flower petals, terracotta idols, woven mats, and cane seating.
* **Textures:** Soft cotton fabrics, matte earth surfaces, antique brass sheen, fresh marigold and lotus textures.

---

### 5.2 Visual Composition Rules
1. **Symmetry & Balance:** Emulate traditional Indian shrine and architectural layouts (symmetrical floral hangings, centered seating, balanced side props).
2. **Human Presence (Inspirational):** Capture candid family moments—assembling decor together, lighting a lamp, laughing, placing the final floral garland.
3. **Detail Close-Ups:** Highlight the richness of backdrop cloth prints, fine line art (Muggu/Kolam motifs), and eco-conscious material finishes.

## 7. Eco-Conscious & DIY Positioning Guidelines

### 7.1 Eco-Conscious Core Pillars
1. **Cloth Over Vinyl/Plastic:** All primary backdrops are printed on high-durability, washable, reusable fabric textiles that drape naturally and eliminate synthetic glare.
2. **Reusable Prop Systems:** Modular wooden, brass-style, and fabric props designed to be rented or reused across multiple festive occasions.
3. **Minimal Packaging Footprint:** Flat-packed shipping systems designed to reduce transit carbon emissions while keeping kits compact and lightweight.

---

### 7.2 DIY Reassurance Messaging
* **Reassure, Don't Overwhelm:** Remind customers that setup requires **zero tools, zero technical skills, and minimal time**.
* **Framing the Setup as a Ritual:** Position assembling the decor as a meaningful family ritual—an enjoyable part of the celebration itself rather than a chore.
